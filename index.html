Symposium Abstract / Resumen del simposio

In this transdisciplinary symposium we begin to think through the frictions that arise from working between the world of science-making and the political, ecological, territorial, and communal struggles.

Friction is the generative force that resists motion, makes traction and forward motion possible, and connotes the tension and conflict caused by clashing ideas or interests. Thinking and working across the disciplines of the arts, architecture, design, ecology, geography, and the political and other struggles of peoples, we want to begin thinking not from (assumed) shared premises but instead from the dissonances, clashes, and contestation between oftentimes opposing and not necessarily aligned agendas and ends of actors operating in the above territories.

With a primary focus on methodological questions, the symposium is organized in a series of workshops, seminars, lectures, and activities. Doing so allows us to engage with concrete struggles, the ethical and philosophical implications that arise when science-making faces communal struggle, and the personal histories and lived experiences that each of us holds.

This symposium is organized between ARQDIS at Universidad de Los Andes and the Manchester School of Architecture at both Manchester Metropolitan University and the University of Manchester. Set participants include academics, post-graduate students from Universidad de Los Andes, the University of Salford, and the Manchester School of Architecture. Students and academics from partaking universities, as well as those who are not from these universities but have an interest in the questions explored are encouraged to apply for participation and contribution. We particularly welcome applications from those who can contribute with a workshop, a paper, a visual artwork, poetry, or any other form of speech they consider relevant.
