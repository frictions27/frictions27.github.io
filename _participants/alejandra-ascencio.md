Alejandra Ascencio is a statistician and Master of Design from Universidad de los Andes in Bogotá, Colombia. She works at the Office of the Ombudsman of Colombia (Defensoría del Pueblo) as a data visualizer. Her research and professional experience sit at the intersection of data analysis, information design, the documentation of urban dynamics, and the management of cultural data for decision-making. Her work focuses on exploring alternative methodologies for data collection and visualization.
