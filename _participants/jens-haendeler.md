Jens Haendeler has been a lecturer at the Manchester School of Architecture since 2024. From 2017 to 2022, he directed the Urban Studies & Spatial Practices program at Al-Quds Bard College in Palestine, where he collaborated with Dr. Shourideh Molavi, the unit lead for Palestine investigations at Forensic Architecture. From 2022 to 2024, he worked on landscape planning, policy, and project steering at IGA 2027. He holds chartership with the Chamber of Architects in Germany and serves as a trustee of the Landscape Research Group.
