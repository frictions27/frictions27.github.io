Purva Dewoolkar is a Bicentenary Research Fellow in Geography at the University of Manchester, with a background spanning architecture, action research, and academic-activist collaboration. Her research explores everyday life around water and sanitation infrastructure in Mumbai's self-built settlements. She is a longstanding convener of Pani Haq Samiti, a collective campaigning for the right to clean drinking water across 72 informal communities in Mumbai. She has published in *Urban Studies*, *Environment and Planning E*, and *City*.
