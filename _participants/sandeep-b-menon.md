Sandeep B. Menon is a Manchester-based landscape architect and academic with experience in practice, teaching, and research across India and the UK. He is part of the Editorial Advisory Panel of *Landscape*, the quarterly journal of the UK Landscape Institute, and a member of the ISOLA Education Board in India. Trained at the School of Planning and Architecture Delhi, he has held academic and leadership roles at KRVIA, University of Mumbai, JDAP Mumbai, and Integral Designs International Studio, working across landscape design and masterplanning projects in diverse biogeographies. His work and research focus on natural processes, local ecology, native planting, ecological urbanism, socio-ecological resilience, sustainable urban water management, ecological corridors, and wetland systems.
