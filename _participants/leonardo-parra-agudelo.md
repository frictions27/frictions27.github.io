Leonardo Parra-Agudelo is an Associate Professor at Universidad de los Andes in Bogotá, Colombia. His work is situated at the intersection of the human and more-than-human, and the processes that emerge from their interactions. He is part of the Participatory Design Conference Advisory Board.
