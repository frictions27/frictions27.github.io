Dr Fadi Shayya (Arabic: فادي شيا) is a transdisciplinary scholar, educator, and design strategist who uses architectural and urban research to investigate how societies negotiate spatial and material change. His written, visual, and collaborative research examines the operational lives of infrastructures, landscapes, and technologies, asking how they shape inhabitation, vulnerability, and the making and unmaking of cities.

At the University of Salford, he is Director of the Built and Human Environments Research Centre and Lecturer in Architecture & Urbanism. He previously served as UoA13 REF Impact Co-Lead. He peer-reviews for UKRI, international conferences, and academic journals. His contributions include the UIA World Congress of Architects (2023), World Habitat Day (2024), and the British Council & THINKlab UK–BiH Fellowship Programme (2025). A Fellow of the Royal Geographical Society (FRGS), he also serves on international advisory and editorial boards and is an Affiliate of Lancaster University’s Centre for Science Studies.
