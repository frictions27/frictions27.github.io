we welcome submissions of short papers which contend with the overarching call for the _frictions_ symposium. Papers can be work in progress and should be between 2,500 and 5,000 words in length. Papers should be propositional and make argumentative. We particularly welcome papers that are pr provocative, stem from lived experience in working with, against, or at the frictions between science-making and communal political, social, ecological, artistic, or territorial struggles. The manuscripts will shared with participants prior to presentation in a penal which will serve as an opportunity to test ideas and receive response.

Priority will be given to submission where the author can attend a majority of events during the core days of the symposium Wednesday March 3 to Thursday March 4.

Timeline:

Call for papers closes December 23rd 2026. Results will be communicated by December 31st 2026, and manuscripts are due for submission at least one week in advance of the symposium.
