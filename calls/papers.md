we welcome submissions of short papers which contend with the overarching call for the _frictions_ symposium. Papers can be work in progress and should not exceed 5000 words in length. Papers should be propositional and make argumentative. We particularly welcome papers that are pr provocative, stem from lived experience in working with, against, or at the frictions between science-making and communal political, social, ecological, artistic, or territorial struggles. The manuscripts will shared with participants prior to presentation in a penal which will serve as an opportunity to test ideas and receive response.
