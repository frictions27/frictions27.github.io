We welcome submissions of artworks or audio-visual contributions of any kind that can be shared with the participants of the symposium either in a performance, digitally by display or screening, or by playback. Artworks and audio-visual submissions may include but are not limited to spoken word poetry, short films, reportage or documentary, cartography, drawings, sound-art or videography. Audio-visual contributions and artworks will be screened in a cinema setting on Thursday the 4th of March 2027.

Submissions will benefit from peer review, and sharp critical response.
